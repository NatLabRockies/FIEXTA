Connect the planner folder (the one holding planet.html) once per browser session. "Save plan" then writes plans/name.json and plans/name.png, and "Open plan…" restores a plan together with the sites and surface-model files it was made with, read again from their paths in the folder. For a new plan, select those files under "Sites file" and "Surface model file"; pick them from inside the connected folder so saved plans can find them. Needs Chrome or Edge.

Azimuth in degrees clockwise from north, swept from Az min to Az max (e.g. 300 to 60 crosses north). 0 to 360 is a full scan.
Dual Doppler: slant range from each lidar to each height z. Single lidar: horizontal distance.
Blockage: along each azimuth (1° steps) the range is cut at the first distance where the surface rises above the beam. The beam leaves at the given height above the ground at the lidar, at the given elevation angle, including Earth curvature with standard 4/3-radius refraction [Doviak and Zrnić, 1993]. Surface: inside the surface-model area (data/dsm.png, made by make_dsm.py), the highest non-noise return per ~2 m pixel of the USGS 3DEP lidar point cloud, so buildings, trees and towers at survey time are included (2 m along-beam steps). Outside it, bare-earth AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen/Tilezen Terrarium), sourced in the contiguous US from USGS 3DEP 1/3 arc-second (~10 m, 1/9 arc-second in places) and elsewhere from SRTM/GMTED (10 m steps). Ground elevations and beam heights above ground always use bare earth. Surface points closer to a lidar than its "Ignore obstacles within" distance are skipped, e.g. to drop nearby structures that the lidar will be mounted above or will not actually see. Dashed outlines show the resulting coverage.

Map layer

Height of the surface used for blockage (surface model where available, bare-earth terrain elsewhere), relative to sea level or to the surface at a point you click after "Set zero at point". Colors stretch over the visible map area unless you set the limits; heights outside the limits take the end colors.

Targets

Sites from the sites file have a solid outline. Click the map to add your own targets (dashed outline), drag them to move, and right-click one to remove it. "Clear" removes only your own targets.

Instruments

Type a name, pick a marker and color, press "Place" and click the map. Drag to move; right-click or ✕ to remove.

Comments

Press "Add comment" and click the map, then type the comment in the box that opens. Hover over a comment to read it, click it to edit or delete it, and drag it to move it. A comment left empty is removed when its box closes. Comments are saved in the plan and drawn on the plan PNG.

Map

Error model: linear error propagation for two-lidar retrieval of horizontal wind with w ≈ 0, independent line-of-sight noise, and shared vertical-velocity contamination [Stawiarski et al., 2013]. Units: σU, σV, σWS in m s⁻¹; σWD in degrees.
